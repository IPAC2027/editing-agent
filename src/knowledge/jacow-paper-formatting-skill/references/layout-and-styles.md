## Page and columns
- Page size 595 pt x 792 pt (20.99 cm x 27.94 cm; 8.264 in x 11.0 in).
- Single-spaced text in two columns of 82.5 mm (3.25 in), separation 5.3 mm (0.2 in). Full text width is 170 mm.
- Margins: Top 19 mm (0.75 in); Bottom 19 mm (0.75 in); Left 20 mm (0.79 in); Right 20 mm (0.79 in).
- Table rules: top/bottom rules 1 pt, mid-rule 0.5 pt.
- Title and author list span the full page width. Tables and figures may span both columns but then go at the top or bottom of a page.
- No page numbers.

## Fonts
- Only Times or Times New Roman (standard, bold, italic) and Symbol. Body 10 pt.
- Liberation Mono for DOIs and URLs in references, 8 pt. If missing, install it (Windows: C:\Windows\Fonts, admin needed).
- Figures: use the same fonts where possible and embed them; non-approved fonts in figures cause processing problems.
- Embed fonts: Word Options -> Save -> "Embed fonts in file".

## Title and heading rules
- Title: 14 pt bold UPPERCASE, centered. Lowercase individual letters to avoid misreading (Au, mW, GeV, SPring-8, SwissFEL). Funding statement: asterisk after the title, text in the bottom-of-column-1 text box.
- Abstract title: 12 pt Initial Caps, italic.
- Section headings: not numbered, 12 pt bold UPPERCASE, centered in column, directly above text.
- Subsection headings: not numbered, 12 pt italic, Title Case, left aligned.
- Third-level headings: 10 pt bold, Initial Caps, run into paragraph text; insert an Em Space after (Word: Insert -> Symbol -> Special Characters -> Em Space).
- Never a column break between a heading and its following paragraph.

## Paragraphs and lists
- 10 pt, justified, first line indented 0.33 cm (0.13 in).
- Do not leave the last line of a paragraph alone at the top of a column, or the first line alone at the bottom.
- Bulleted/numbered lists: left indent 0.33 cm, hanging 0.36 cm (text says 0.3 cm in one place; Table 2 says 0.33 cm), spacing 0 pt before, 3 pt after.

## Figures and tables
- Place as close as possible after first mention. Number consecutively (1, 2, 3...) through the paper.
- After inserting a figure: right click -> Wrap Text -> "In Line with Text".
- Figure captions BELOW figures; table captions ABOVE tables.
- Caption text 10 pt normal, format "Figure 1: ..." / "Table 1: ..." (colon after number).
- Single-line captions centered; multi-line captions justified (the guide's Fig. 2 shows left-justified multi-line).
- Table captions normally a heading: initial caps of principal words, no final period. Refer to table contents in body text, not in the caption. If a full-sentence caption is needed: sentence case with a period.
- Insert captions via References tab -> Insert Caption, choose label Figure/Table, set position (below for figure, above for table), then fix font (10 pt normal) and spacing.
- Spanning two columns:
  1. Insert a continuous section break.
  2. Insert two empty lines.
  3. Insert another continuous section break.
  4. Click between the breaks: Page Layout -> Columns -> One.
  5. Paste the figure between the breaks; resize.
  6. Remove blank lines in and around the figure.
- In text: "Fig. 1" (abbreviated) except at sentence start ("Figure 1"). "Table 1" never abbreviated. Avoid starting sentences with abbreviations or numbers.

## Equations
- Number only if referenced. Number in parentheses, flush right in the column. Equation itself indented and centered as far as possible.
- Equation paragraph: 10 pt base font; space before/after at least 6 pt.
- Reference as "Eq. (1)" in text, "Equation (1)" at sentence start.

## Units
- Upright font (variables italic). Non-breaking space before the unit: 3 keV, 100 kW, 7 um (use the proper micro sign).
- Hyphenated compound adjective before a noun: singular unit (the 3.8-metre long undulator).

## Footnotes
- Do NOT use Word's Insert -> Footnote.
- Title/author footnotes (acknowledgements, e-mail): symbols in sequence * dagger double-dagger section pilcrow. Placed manually in a text box at the bottom of column 1 on page 1, with a separator line at the top of the box. Easiest: copy the text box from the JACoW template. Text must stay inside column margins.
- Body footnotes: numeric 1, 2, 3, same text-box technique, at the bottom of the column where used.
- All footnotes 8 pt.

## Style summary table (Table 2 of the guide)
| Style | Font | Space before | Space after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper Title | 14 pt, UPPERCASE (except required lowercase), bold | 0 | 3 pt |
| Author List | 12 pt, upper/lowercase | 9 pt | 12 pt |
| Abstract Title | 12 pt, Initial Caps, italic | 0 | 3 pt |
| Section Heading | 12 pt, UPPERCASE, bold | 9 pt | 3 pt |
| Subsection Heading | 12 pt, Initial Caps, italic | 6 pt | 3 pt |
| Third-level Heading | 10 pt, Initial Caps, bold | 6 pt | 0 |
| Figure Caption | 10 pt | 3 pt | >= 3 pt |
| Table Caption | 10 pt | >= 3 pt | 3 pt |
| Equation | 10 pt base font | >= 6 pt | >= 6 pt |
| Bulleted/Numbered List | 10 pt, left indent 0.33 cm, hanging 0.36 cm | 0 | 3 pt |
| References (<= 9 refs) | 9 pt justified, hanging 0.52 cm (0.2 in) | 0 | 3 pt |
| References 1-9 (when >= 10) | 9 pt justified, left 0.16 cm (0.06 in), hanging 0.52 cm | 0 | 3 pt |
| References 10 onwards | 9 pt justified, hanging 0.68 cm (0.26 in) | 0 | 3 pt |

Note: headings, table captions and equations at the top of a column need 0 pt space before.

## Section order and misc
- Order: Abstract, body sections, CONCLUSION, ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS, APPENDIX (only if essential; before or after REFERENCES depending on content), REFERENCES.
- Submission: each author submits the PDF plus all source files (text and figures) so the paper can be rebuilt if needed. Follow the conference for page limits and the submission method.
- Templates: https://www.jacow.org
