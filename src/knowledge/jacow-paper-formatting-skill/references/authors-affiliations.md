# Title, authors and affiliations (Annex A)

## General
- 12 pt, upper- and lowercase, centered, full page width. Spacing 9 pt before, 12 pt after.
- Submitting/primary author first, then co-authors alphabetically.
- Keep initials with surname: use non-breaking spaces between initials and last name so a line break never separates them.
- Affiliation: minimal institute name + city, country. Omit post codes unless unique and mandated.
- Footnote marks on author lines: * dagger double-dagger section pilcrow (e.g. e-mail as dagger footnote in the first-column text box).

## Grouped by author (default)
Single affiliation, one line:
A. Alpha, CERN, Geneva, Switzerland

Multiple affiliations: superscript numbers after names, numbered affiliation lines below.
A. Alpha(1,2), B. Beta(3), C. Gamma(4), D. Delta(5)
1 CERN, Geneva, Switzerland
2 Cambridge University, Cambridge, UK
3 Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron DESY, Zeuthen, Germany
4 SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, Menlo Park, CA, USA
5 RIKEN Nishina Center, Wako, Saitama, Japan

## Grouped by affiliation (many affiliations / if mandated)
Each line: author, institute, location. Centered. A secondary affiliation is a superscript on the author and appears below the list as "also at ...":
A. Alpha(1), CERN, Geneva, Switzerland
B. Beta, Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron DESY, Zeuthen, Germany
C. Gamma, SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, Menlo Park, CA, USA
D. Delta, RIKEN Nishina Center, Wako, Saitama, Japan
1 also at Cambridge University, Cambridge, UK

## Large collaborations (tens of authors, page limits)
Preferred: principal author + institute, then "on behalf of the ... collaboration":
A. Alpha, CERN, Geneva, Switzerland
on behalf of the MICE collaboration

If all names are listed explicitly, each author must also be entered in Indico via the web portal so authors are cross-referenced to papers in the final proceedings.
