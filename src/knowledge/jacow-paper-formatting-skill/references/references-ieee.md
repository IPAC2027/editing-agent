# Reference list formatting (IEEE style as applied to JACoW, Annex B)

## General rules
- Numbered, listed at the end under REFERENCES, cited in square brackets in ascending order of first citation. Multiple: [2, 3]; ranges [1-3, 10]. Every entry must be cited.
- Style basis: IEEE Editorial Style Manual (follows Chicago Manual of Style). Good style ensures clean import to INSPIRE, Scopus, Google Scholar.
- Times New Roman 9 pt; DOIs and URLs in Liberation Mono 8 pt. Line spacing exactly 10.4 pt (not "single").
- DOI: append at the end where it exists (strongly mandated), keep it on a single line (no arbitrary breaks), format `doi:10.xxxx/...`, hyperlinked in Blue, Accent 1, 25% darker. With Acrobat PDFMaker enable "Add Links".
- URL: only when there is no DOI; monospaced; NOT hyperlinked (hyperlinks to non-DOI URLs are prohibited).
- Conference paper ID may be given in the absence of a DOI.

## Author lists
| No. authors | Format |
|---|---|
| 1 | A. T. Alpha, |
| 2 | A. T. Alpha and B. Beta, |
| 3 to 6 | A. T. Alpha, B. Beta, and J.-P. Gamma, (comma before "and") |
| > 6 | A. T. Alpha et al., |
| > 6 with two primary authors | A. T. Alpha, B. Beta, et al., |

## Paper titles
Sentence case (only first word capitalised; proper nouns and acronyms keep capitals). Title in double quotes with the comma/period placement shown in the examples (closing quote followed by a comma, no comma inside).

## Proceedings papers
Pattern: Authors, "Title", in Proc. CONF'YY, City, [State,] Country, Mon. Year, pp. X-Y. doi:...
- Conference title in italics, title case with standard abbreviations (Int., Conf.); "in" in normal font before it. Short form is enough: in Proc. IPAC'24. Full name with ISO 4 abbreviations allowed: in Proc. 13th Int. Comput. Accel. Phys. Conf. (ICAP'18).
- Then location (city, state if applicable, country), month (3-letter abbreviation) and year, then pages (mandatory) and DOI.
- Distinguish published papers (have page numbers, recent ones DOIs) from presented-but-unpublished ones.

Examples:
[1] A. Alpha, "Novel techniques for a future TeV electron accelerator", in Proc. IPAC'23, Venice, Italy, May 2023, pp. 57-59. doi:10.18429/JACoW-IPAC2023-PAPERID
[2] A. Alpha and B. T. Beta, "An overview of modern control systems", in Proc. ICALEPCS'23, Cape Town, South Africa, Oct. 2023, pp. 57-59. doi:10.18429/JACoW-ICALEPCS2023-PAPERID
[3] A. Alpha, B. T. Beta, and J.-P. Gamma, "Compact light sources", in Proc. FLS'23, Lucerne, Switzerland, Aug.-Sep. 2023, pp. 57-59. doi:10.18429/JACoW-FLS2023-PAPERID
[4] A. Alpha et al., "Novel techniques for future TeV electron accelerators", in Proc. LINAC'24, Chicago, IL, USA, Aug. 2024, pp. 57-59. doi:10.18429/JACoW-LINAC2024-PAPERID
[5] A. Alpha, B. T. Beta, et al., "Status of cyclotrons around the world", in Proc. Cyclotrons'22, Beijing, China, Dec. 2022, pp. 57-59. doi:10.18429/JACoW-CYCLOTRONS2022-PAPERID

Unpublished paper from a previous conference (conference name in normal font; paper ID if supplementary material such as the talk PDF exists on JACoW):
[6] A. Alpha, "An interesting talk but paper not submitted", presented at IPAC'14, Dresden, Germany, Jun. 2014, paper MOP057, unpublished.

Paper at the current conference (normal font; may append "this conference"):
[7] A. Alpha and B. T. Beta, "Title of talk presented at this conference", presented at IPAC'25, Taipei, Taiwan, Jun. 2025, paper MOAB01, this conference.

## Alignment and fonts
Hanging-indent structure; reference numbers in the first line are right aligned; continuation lines indented.
| Case | Left indent | Hanging indent | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <= 9 references | 0.00 cm | 0.52 cm (0.20 in) | 0 pt | 3 pt |
| >= 10 refs: Refs 1-9 | 0.16 cm (0.06 in) | 0.52 cm | 0 pt | 3 pt |
| >= 10 refs: Refs 10 onwards | 0.00 cm | 0.68 cm (0.26 in) | 0 pt | 3 pt |
All 9 pt justified, line spacing exactly 10.4 pt.

## Conversion checklist when reformatting a bibliography
1. Re-order to citation order; renumber in-text citations to match; drop or cite unused entries.
2. Authors: initials first, correct comma/"and"/"et al." rule.
3. Sentence-case the title; keep acronyms and proper nouns.
4. Proceedings: Proc. ACRONYM'YY, location, Mon. Year, pp. X-Y, DOI. Journals: ISO 4 abbreviation, vol., page/article no., year, DOI.
5. Add DOI hyperlinks (blue) in Liberation Mono 8 pt; remove hyperlinks on plain URLs.
6. Apply the hanging-indent values for the reference count; line spacing exactly 10.4 pt.
